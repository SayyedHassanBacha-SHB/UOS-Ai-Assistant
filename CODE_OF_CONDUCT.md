# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in the University of Swat AI Assistant project.

We welcome contributions from people of different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

All project participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Welcome suggestions, feedback, and different opinions.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors' time and efforts.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, insulting, or inappropriate language.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sharing confidential information, credentials, or sensitive data.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Project Responsibilities

Contributors should:

- Respect the project's technical guidelines.
- Avoid submitting malicious or harmful code.
- Preserve the privacy and security of users.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property rights.
- Provide accurate sources when contributing university information.
- Avoid submitting misleading or fabricated university information.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer.

Please use the project's available private communication or GitHub contact options.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriately as possible.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity of the behavior, actions may include:

- A warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Removal from the project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related communication spaces.

## Acknowledgement

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and its principles of respectful open-source collaboration.
