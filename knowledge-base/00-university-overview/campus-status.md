# Campus Locations and Recognition Status

## Main Campus

The official Main Campus page identifies the Main Campus as **Charbagh, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan**. It lists academic blocks, teaching departments and administrative offices located at the Main Campus.

Official page: https://uswat.edu.pk/maincampus/

## Women Campus

The University of Swat maintains a Women Campus page. The page states that the Women Campus was inaugurated on **25 July 2017** and describes the College of Home Sciences as a constituent college whose Home Economics department became part of the Women Campus.

The Women Campus page lists these departments:
- Botany
- Psychological Studies
- Zoology
- Home Economics
- Computer and Software Technology
- English and Foreign Languages

The page identifies a Campus Coordinator and provides the email `mahrukhanum@uswat.edu.pk`. It also lists `womencampus@uswat.edu.pk` as a contact email and refers to the Girls Hostel at Main Campus.

Official page: https://uswat.edu.pk/women-campus/

## HEC campus-list caveat

The HEC recognised-campuses page lists **Women Campus Swat** with a validity period shown as **15 January 2024 to 30 September 2025**. Since that displayed end date has passed, this knowledge base does **not** assert that the campus currently holds the same HEC campus recognition status. Users asking about current recognition should be directed to HEC and the university for confirmation.

## Shangla

The University of Swat's historical About page says a Shangla Campus was established. However, the HEC recognised-campuses page states that **Shangla was upgraded to the University of Shangla with effect from 1 August 2022**.

Therefore, do not describe Shangla as a current University of Swat campus. The historical reference should be understood in the context of the later upgrade.

## How the chatbot should answer campus questions

- For the university's currently presented campus information, link to the official Main Campus and Women Campus pages.
- If asked whether Women Campus is currently HEC-recognised, disclose that the HEC page currently displays a validity period ending 30 September 2025 and recommend confirmation from HEC/the university.
- If asked about Shangla, explain that it was historically mentioned as a University of Swat campus and that HEC records its upgrade to the University of Shangla effective 1 August 2022.
- Do not infer current campus operations, admission availability, program offerings or recognition from an old page alone.
