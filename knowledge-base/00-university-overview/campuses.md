# Campuses

| Campus / location | What the sources support | Current-status note |
|---|---|---|
| Main Campus | The University of Swat's official Main Campus page identifies Charbagh, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. | Presented by the university as its Main Campus. Facilities and programme availability must be checked on current official pages. |
| Women Campus | The university maintains a Women Campus page. It states the campus was inaugurated on 25 July 2017 and lists six departments: Botany, Psychological Studies, Zoology, Home Economics, Computer and Software Technology, and English and Foreign Languages. | HEC's recognised-campuses page displays a validity period from 15 January 2024 to 30 September 2025. Because the displayed end date has passed, this knowledge base does not assert current HEC recognition. Confirm directly with HEC and the university. |
| Shangla | The university's historical About page mentions a Shangla Campus. | HEC states Shangla was upgraded to the University of Shangla effective 1 August 2022. Do not describe it as a current University of Swat campus. |

## Important address distinction
The Main Campus page identifies Charbagh. The HEC institutional profile separately displays G.T. Road, Odigram as contact information. These are recorded as different source contexts and must not be merged into one address without confirmation.

## Chatbot response guidance
- For Main Campus location, use Charbagh and link to the official Main Campus page.
- For Women Campus questions, distinguish the university's published page from HEC's dated recognition entry. Do not claim current HEC recognition based only on a validity period ending 30 September 2025.
- For Shangla, explain the historical relationship and HEC-recorded upgrade to the University of Shangla on 1 August 2022.
- Do not infer current admissions, programmes, facilities, operational status or recognition from historical or undated pages.
