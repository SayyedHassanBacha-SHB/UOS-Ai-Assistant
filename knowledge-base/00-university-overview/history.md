# History of the University of Swat

## Key dated facts (as stated on the official About page)
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2010-05-29 | The then Prime Minister of Pakistan announced the establishment of the University of Swat during a visit to the Swat valley. |
| 2010-07 (July 2010) | The university came into formal existence by order of the Governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. |
| 2010-08 (August 2010) | First Vice Chancellor, Dr. Muhammad Farooq Khan (Shaheed), was appointed. |
| Academic session 2010-11 | The first academic session; 7 departments were launched. |

## Early infrastructure
The Higher Education Department handed over two buildings at Saidu Sharif, Swat, to the new university. Land for the university's own campus at Charbagh was acquired. The About page says the Main Campus now resides at Charbagh.

## Growth
The About page says departments increased from 7 to 27 and that two new campuses, a Woman Campus and a Shangla Campus, were established.

## Conflicts and caveats
- **Conflicting department counts:** the About page says departments grew to 27; the homepage paragraph (2017 snapshot) says 22; the website navigation on 2026-11-18 lists 24 teaching departments. These describe different dates or counting methods. Use the navigation list in `02-academics/departments.md` for the current published list.
- **Shangla:** historical University of Swat material mentions a Shangla Campus. HEC states that Shangla was upgraded to the University of Shangla with effect from 1 August 2022. Do not list it as a current University of Swat campus.
- **Women Campus recognition:** the university website continues to present a Women Campus page, while HEC's recognised-campuses page shows a validity period ending 30 September 2025. Current recognition status requires confirmation.
