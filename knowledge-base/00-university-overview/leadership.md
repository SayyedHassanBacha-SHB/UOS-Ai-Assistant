# Leadership

## Vice Chancellor
- **Prof. Dr. Husnul Amin**, Vice Chancellor, University of Swat (as displayed on the homepage on 2026-11-18).
- The homepage carries a "Vice Chancellor's Message" (https://uswat.edu.pk/vice-chancellors-message-2/). In it the Vice Chancellor says he is taking up office and expresses the ambition of making the university a nationally recognised and internationally connected centre of academic excellence, research innovation and societal engagement. The date of the message was not shown in the retrieved text.
