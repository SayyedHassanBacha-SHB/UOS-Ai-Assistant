# Legal Establishment, Public-Sector Status and HEC Recognition

## Legal establishment

The Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's official law record identifies the **University of Swat Regulation, 2010 (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Regulation No. II of 2010)**. The regulation is dated **7 July 2010** and provides for the establishment of the University of Swat.

The university's own About University page separately states that its establishment was announced on 29 May 2010 and that it came into formal existence in July 2010.

## Public-sector status and recognition

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) institution profile identifies the University of Swat as:
- Sector: Public
- Chartered by: Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
- Established since: 7 July 2010
- Recognised University: Yes
- City: Swat
- Province: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

For current recognition details, refer directly to the HEC profile because HEC recognition and campus status can change.

## Address discrepancy to preserve

The University of Swat website identifies the **Main Campus as Charbagh, Swat**. The HEC institution profile displays contact information at **G.T. Road, Odigram, Swat**. These may represent different postal/contact locations. Do not merge them into one address or tell users they are identical without confirmation from the university.
