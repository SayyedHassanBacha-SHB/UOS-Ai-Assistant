# University Overview - FAQs

**Question:** When was the University of Swat established?
**Answer:** The official About page says the establishment was announced on 29 May 2010 and the university came into formal existence in July 2010 by order of the Governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/about-university/

**Question:** Where is the University of Swat's main campus?
**Answer:** At Charbagh, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. All main academic campuses and administrative offices are there, per the About page.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/about-university/

**Question:** Who is the Vice Chancellor?
**Answer:** The homepage displayed Prof. Dr. Husnul Amin as Vice Chancellor on 2026-11-18. Check the website for any later change.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/

**Question:** Is the University of Swat a public or private university?
**Answer:** Public-sector, per the About page.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/about-university/

**Question:** Is the University of Swat the same as the University of Agriculture Swat, the University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences Swat, or other Swat institutions?
**Answer:** No. Third-party admission listings show these as separate institutions in Swat with their own admissions. This answer rests on external listings, not an official University of Swat statement. When answering admission questions, make sure the source refers to the University of Swat (uswat.edu.pk).
**Source:** https://www.pakadmissions.com/admissions-in-swat (external)

**Question:** How many students and programs does the university have today?
**Answer:** Not available. The homepage counters could not be read, and the only published figures found are from 2017. Refer users to the university website.
