# University of Swat - Profile

**Official name:** University of Swat
**Motto/tagline shown on website:** "Aspiring for Quality Education with Relevance"
**Type:** Public-sector university (stated on the official About page).
**Location of main campus and administrative offices:** Main Campus, Charbagh, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.
**Official website:** https://uswat.edu.pk/
**Current Vice Chancellor (as shown on the homepage on 2026-11-18):** Prof. Dr. Husnul Amin.

## Summary
The University of Swat was created to provide higher education to the people of the Swat Valley and the wider country. The About page says it was established in 2010. Its main academic campus and all administrative offices are at Charbagh, Swat.

## Structure at a glance (from website navigation, 2026-11-18)
- Four faculty groupings of teaching departments (24 departments in total), plus a Faculty of Allied Health Sciences and the Hunerkada Center of Excellence for Art and Culture. See `02-academics/faculties.md` and `02-academics/departments.md`.
- The university website presents Main Campus (Charbagh) and Women Campus. HEC's campus list displays a Women Campus recognition period ending 30 September 2025; current recognition status should be confirmed. Shangla was upgraded to the University of Shangla effective 1 August 2022 according to HEC. See `00-university-overview/campus-status.md`.
- The official website publishes a vision, mission, six core values and objectives. See `00-university-overview/vision-mission-values.md`.
- Research office (ORIC), Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC), and a set of administrative offices and directorates. See `05-administration/`.

## Statistics - status: unverified
The homepage shows animated counters for campuses, undergraduate programs, MS/MPhil programs, doctorate programs, faculty and students. The values were not readable in the retrieved page (they appeared as 0 because they load dynamically). **No current counts are recorded here.**

The homepage "About University" paragraph gives an older snapshot: 7 disciplines and 272 students at the start, growing to 22 teaching departments with 49 academic programs including 2 sub-campuses, with more than 3,000 students enrolled in 2017. This is **historical (2017)** and must not be presented as current.

## Recognition and address notes
- HEC identifies University of Swat as a recognised public university, chartered by the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
- The university's Main Campus page says Charbagh, Swat. HEC's profile displays G.T. Road, Odigram, Swat as contact information. Do not assume these are the same address.
