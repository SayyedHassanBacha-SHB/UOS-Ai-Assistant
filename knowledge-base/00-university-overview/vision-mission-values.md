# Vision, Mission, Core Values and Objectives

The University of Swat's official About University page publishes the following statements.

## Vision

> The University of Swat aspires to be a premier seat of learning, distinctively recognized for transformative education and multi-disciplinary research to serve humanity.

## Mission statement

> University of Swat is committed to provide academic and rewarding environment for intellectual growth to promote quality research and education, focused on creative and critical thinking with multi-cultural affinity of glorious human values.

## Core values

The official page lists six core values:

1. Commitment
2. Creativity
3. Excellence
4. Responsibility
5. Leadership
6. Service

## Objectives

The official page lists objectives concerning:
- Recognizing and rewarding academic merit.
- Nurturing creativity and academic excellence.
- Providing students and academia with a level playing field for intellectual and cultural development.
- Supporting professional career advancement and social and moral development.
- Encouraging human-resource development for stakeholders.
- Developing young people as productive global citizens.
- Supporting career and intellectual growth in a diverse environment.

**Editorial note:** The objective bullets above are concise paraphrases. Consult the official page for its full original wording.

## Motto

**“Aspiring for Quality Education with Relevance”**
