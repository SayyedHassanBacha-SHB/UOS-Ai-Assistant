# Admission FAQs

**Question:** How can I contact the University of Swat admissions office?
**Answer:** Email admissions@uswat.edu.pk. The university Contact List shows the Admission Office telephone as 920809 (the list does not give an area code). The Director Admissions is Mr. Fida Hussain.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/directorate-of-admissions/ ; https://uswat.edu.pk/contact-list/

**Question:** Can a student get a refund of tuition fee after withdrawing?
**Answer:** The University Statutes 2025 say students who cancel or withdraw may be entitled to a refund of tuition fee on listed grounds (for example personal emergencies, health reasons, family medical care, legal or immigration matters, delay in admission elsewhere), decided case by case, by applying on the prescribed form with proof of payment. The Syndicate can declare some fees non-refundable, and expelled students are not eligible except for the refundable security deposit.
**Source:** University of Swat Statutes, 2025 (Fees and other Miscellaneous Charges Statutes), https://uswat.edu.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/PDF-Approved-Final-Statutes-2025-Searchable-1.pdf

**Question:** How much does a DMC, transcript or degree cost?
**Answer:** Under the Syndicate-approved revised fee schedule (about September 2024): DMC normal Rs. 800; original transcript Rs. 700; degree fee normal Rs. 3,500. Other items and urgent rates are in `fee-structure.md`. This schedule may be outdated, so confirm with the university.
**Source:** https://uswat.edu.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Revised-Schedule-of-fee-Approved-by-syndicate.pdf

**Question:** What are the eligibility requirements and the merit formula for undergraduate admission?
**Answer:** Not available in this knowledge base. The official admission tabs could not be read. Refer the user to the Directorate of Admissions page.
