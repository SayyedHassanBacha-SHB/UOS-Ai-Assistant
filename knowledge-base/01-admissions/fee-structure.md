# Fee Structure and Fee Rules

> **Important scope note:** the only fee schedule that could be read is the Syndicate-approved *Revised Schedule of Fee* (reference UoS/Meetings/57-SYND/2024-15, uploaded around September 2024). It lists **registration, examination, certificate and administrative fees**. **Tuition and admission fees for individual programs were not found.** This schedule is **potentially outdated** for the current session (2026). Users should confirm with the university. Amounts are in Pakistani rupees (Rs.).

## Fee rules from the University of Swat Statutes, 2025 (Fees and other Miscellaneous Charges Statutes, pp. 32-34 of the PDF)
- **Fee categories (other than hostel fees):** admission fee, tuition fee, medical fee, examination fee, university registration fee, contributions to specified educational, social and recreational funds, and other fees set from time to time. The university may also charge magazine fee, Students' Welfare Fund fee, identity card fee and others decided by the Syndicate.
- **Who sets fees:** the Syndicate, on the recommendations of the Academic Council and the Finance and Planning Committee.
- **Annual increase:** the Syndicate may make a 10% annual increase in fees and charges, or increase/decrease the rate. An increase applies to **newly admitted students**; already-admitted students continue paying the fee in force at first admission unless the Syndicate decides otherwise in a particular case with written reasons.
- **Refund:** students who cancel or withdraw may be entitled to a tuition fee refund on grounds such as personal emergencies, health reasons, care or medical reasons for family members, legal/immigration matters, delay in admission in other universities, and other cases decided case by case. A student required to leave because entry requirements were not met after results are announced is eligible for a refund. Applications need the prescribed form and proof of payment. The Syndicate may declare any fee non-refundable. A student expelled in disciplinary proceedings gets no refund except the refundable security deposit.
- **Fee adjustment on a second admission:** if a student is admitted to another discipline, program or department within 10 days of the first admission, fees already deposited transfer to the new department; any shortfall is paid and any excess refunded.
