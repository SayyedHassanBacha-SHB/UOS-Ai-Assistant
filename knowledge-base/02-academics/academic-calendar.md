## Key finding
No academic calendar for 2025 or 2026 is posted on the Academic Calendar page. The contents of the three calendar files were **not analysed** in this stage, so no semester start/end dates are recorded here.

## Guidance
Do not give semester dates from older calendars as current. Direct users to https://uswat.edu.pk/academic-calendar/ and department notices.

## What is confirmed about the academic year
The University Statutes 2025 define the academic year as two semesters, **Fall and Spring**.
