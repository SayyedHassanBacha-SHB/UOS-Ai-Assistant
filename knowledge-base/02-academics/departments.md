## Naming differences between sources
The university Contact List uses some different unit names from the navigation menu. Treat them as possibly the same unit, but this is **unverified**:
- Navigation: Department of Psychological Studies / Contact List: Department of Psychological Sciences
- Navigation: Department of Chemistry / Contact List: Institute of Chemical Sciences
- Navigation: Department of Physics / Contact List: Department of Physical and Material Sciences
- Navigation: Department of Management & Commerce / Contact List: Center for Management and Commerce
- Navigation: Department of Tourism and Hospitality / Contact List: ICHTHM (Tourism Department)
- Contact List also names: Centre for Caucasian, Asian, Chinese and Pakistan Studies; Center for Plant Sciences (Botany); Center for Biotechnology & Microbiology; Center for Animal Sciences and Fisheries; Department of Forensic Sciences; Institute of Agriculture Sciences and Forestry.

## Counting caution

Do not tell users that the university has exactly 24 departments without qualification. The current navigation lists 24 teaching department entries under four faculty headings, while the About page's historical text says the number grew to 27, and separate campus/faculty pages publish additional or differently grouped units. These may reflect different dates or administrative groupings.
