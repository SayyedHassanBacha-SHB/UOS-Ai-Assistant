# Faculty of Allied Health Sciences

The University of Swat's Faculty of Allied Health Sciences page states that establishment of the faculty was approved in the 9th meeting of the University Senate.

## Departments listed by the faculty page

1. Department for Medical Technologies
2. Department of Nursing and Public Health
3. Department for Pharmaceutical and Rehabilitation Sciences
4. Department for Food and Nutrition Sciences

## Degree programmes listed by the faculty page

- BS Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT)
- BS Dental Technology
- Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT)
- BS Radiography and Imaging Technology

**Important:** This is a list published on the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences page, not a guarantee that every programme is accepting applications in the current admission cycle. Check the current admission advertisement/prospectus before advising applicants.
