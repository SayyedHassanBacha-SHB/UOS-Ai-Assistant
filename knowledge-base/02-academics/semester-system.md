# Semester System

## Confirmed
- The Statutes 2025 define an "academic year" as a period of two semesters: **Fall Semester and Spring Semester**.
- Statutes on teacher recruitment treat semester-system results using a CGPA scale out of 4.00, and say a CGPA of 3.00 or greater out of 4.00 (or Grade B or greater) is treated as equivalent to a first division for those purposes. This is about staff recruitment eligibility, not student progression.
