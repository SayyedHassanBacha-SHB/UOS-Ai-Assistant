# Official Digital Services and University Portals

The University of Swat website links to several digital services. Some services require an account and authentication.

## Campus Management System (CMS)

Official portal: https://cms1.uswat.edu.pk/

The publicly visible CMS landing page lists these service areas:
- Admission Management System
- Enrollment and Registration Management System
- Student Portal
- Faculty Portal
- Hostel Management System
- Examinations Management System
- Scholarship Management System
- Fee Management System
- Alumni Management System

The portal provides registration for new admission applicants and login for existing staff, faculty, students and applicants.

## Examination portal

The university's website links to the examination portal:

https://exam.uswat.edu.pk/

The portal hosts examination-related pages and downloadable forms. Use the live portal for current notices, forms and examination information.

## Other online services listed by the university

The official website navigation also lists:
- e-Office
- Office 365
- Digital Transformation of Payment
- Student Loan Schemes
- Digital Library
- Pakistan Qualification Register (PQR)
- M-Varsities

These names are a directory of services, not proof that every service is currently operational or publicly accessible.

## Privacy and chatbot handling

- The public chatbot may explain what a portal is for and provide its official link.
- The chatbot must not ask users to provide passwords, CNICs, roll numbers, examination credentials or other sensitive identifiers in ordinary chat.
- Do not attempt to retrieve private records from CMS or examination systems without an approved university integration and appropriate authentication.
- Do not imply that the chatbot can access a student's portal account unless such an integration has been formally implemented and authorized.
