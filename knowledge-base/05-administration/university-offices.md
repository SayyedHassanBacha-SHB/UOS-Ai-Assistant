## Governance bodies named in the Statutes 2025
Senate, Syndicate, Academic Council, Finance and Planning Committee, Advanced Studies and Research Board, Selection Board, Board of Faculties and Board of Studies are defined terms in the Statutes (pp. 5-12). The Statutes say they are made under the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Universities Act, 2012, and define the Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, Treasurer, Controller of Examinations, Provost, Director Academics, Director ORIC and Director QEC as university offices.
