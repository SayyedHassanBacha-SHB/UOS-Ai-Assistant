# ORIC - Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization

## Confirmed facts
- ORIC page: https://uswat.edu.pk/oric-2/ (not retrieved).
- A Syndicate decision listed on Downloads approved a change in nomenclature from the **Directorate of Outreach, Research, Innovation and Commercialization (DORIC)** to the **Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization (ORIC)** (file uploaded January 2021; the document was not read).
- The Statutes 2025 define "ORIC" as the Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization, and refer to a Director ORIC.
- Homepage announcements show ORIC organised an awareness and training session for PhD faculty members (news dated 2026-11-02).

## Research-related policies listed on Downloads (61st Syndicate meeting; not read)
Implementation of Technology Transfer Policy; Implementation of Research Ethics Policy; Implementation of Research Conflict of Interest Policy.

## Other research units
- Advanced Studies and Research Board (ASRB): https://uswat.edu.pk/advanced-studies-and-research-board/
- SUJRIS journal site: https://sujris.uswat.edu.pk/
- Homepage news in 2026 mentions MoUs relating to Gandhara civilization research (University of Swat and Sri Lankan NIIBS, 2026-11-06) and an HEC-NRPU funded research project job advertisement (2026-09-11).
