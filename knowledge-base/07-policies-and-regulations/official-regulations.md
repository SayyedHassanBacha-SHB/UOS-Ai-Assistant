## Content of Statutes 2025 that was read (pages 1-64)
- **Applicability and governance:** the Statutes apply to authorities, faculties, departments, directorates, centres, institutes and sub-campuses, and to university employees (except contract, deputation and contingency staff).
- **Service rules:** probation of one year (extendable by one year); retirement on superannuation at 60 years; annual increment on 1 December; right of appeal within 30 days. These concern employees, not students.
- **Fees and miscellaneous charges:** see `01-admissions/fee-structure.md`.
- **Register of Registered Graduates:** the Registrar maintains it through the Controller of Examinations, and a report of the number of registered graduates is published on the university website.
- **Annual Report:** prepared by the university, considered by the Syndicate and submitted to the Senate within three months of the end of the academic year.
- **Teacher recruitment:** vacancies are advertised in at least two nationwide newspapers and on the university website; selection board interview qualifying marks are 60%; teacher scales are BPS-18 Lecturer, BPS-19 Assistant Professor, BPS-20 Associate Professor, BPS-21 Professor, BPS-22 Meritorious Professor; closing date normally 15 to 30 days after advertisement.
