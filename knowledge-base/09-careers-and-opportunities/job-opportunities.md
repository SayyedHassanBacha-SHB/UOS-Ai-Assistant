## Recruitment rules (Statutes 2025)
Teaching posts are advertised in at least two nationwide newspapers and on the university website. Applications must be on the prescribed format with the prescribed fee. Syndicate decisions listed on Downloads mention a 5% minority quota and a 2% disability quota in recruitment (61st Syndicate meeting; documents not read).
