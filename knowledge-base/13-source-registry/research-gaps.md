# Research Gaps and Coverage Report

**Knowledge-base version:** 2.0 (reconciliation and RAG-readiness pass)  
**Audit date:** 2026-11-18  
**Scope:** supplied University of Swat archive and the official-source references recorded in it. This update reconciles documentation; it does not claim that every university page was freshly crawled during this pass.

## Coverage available in this archive

- University overview, history, leadership snapshot, vision, mission, core values and objectives.
- Legal establishment and HEC institutional recognition details.
- Main Campus and Women Campus descriptions, plus HEC campus-status caveats for Women Campus and Shangla.
- Faculty and department names, including the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences; directory completeness is not established.
- Admissions contacts and general information, fee schedule snapshot, scholarships notice/forms and deadline status.
- Academic calendar snapshot, semester-system material and selected regulations.
- Student-service portal descriptions, administrative offices, contact directories and ORIC references.
- News/jobs snapshot, official documents inventory, source registry and chatbot-use cautions.

## Resolved documentation gaps from the earlier report

The previous report incorrectly continued to list the Main Campus and Women Campus pages as not retrieved, and said the About-page vision/mission tabs were inaccessible. The updated archive contains `campus-status.md`, `vision-mission-values.md` and the Allied Health Sciences supplement. These topics are therefore no longer listed as wholly unresearched. Their facts still have limits: current recognition and operational details must be checked where indicated.

The earlier statement that Shangla's status was unverified is superseded by the HEC entry recorded in `campus-status.md`: Shangla was upgraded to the University of Shangla effective 1 August 2022. Do not carry forward the old uncertainty statement.

## Remaining gaps and limitations

### Admissions and programmes
- No complete, current prospectus with programme-by-programme eligibility, merit criteria, entry-test requirements, required documents, seats, duration and credit hours is established in this archive.
- No current admission deadline is verified. `01-admissions/admission-deadlines.md` remains unverified.
- Programme availability can change by admission cycle; department existence does not prove a programme is currently offered.

### Fees and scholarships
- The fee schedule is a dated snapshot (approximately 2024 in the source material) and may have been superseded. Do not quote it as the current fee.
- Current programme-specific tuition/admission fees are not established.
- Scholarship coverage is incomplete; some notices/forms are old, and the image-only KPEF notice details were not fully extracted.

### Academics and regulations
- The newest academic calendar identified in the source set is from 2024; a current 2025-2026 or later calendar is not established.
- Three semester-regulation PDFs were reported as scanned without a text layer; OCR and verification remain outstanding.
- The University Statutes 2025 were only partially analysed (approximately 64 of 368 pages in the original research notes).
- Grading, examination, attendance, repeat-course and CGPA rules should not be answered unless the relevant current regulation text is available and cited.

### Departments, staff and facilities
- Department and faculty listings are not a complete verified programme directory or full faculty directory.
- Department counts and groupings differ across navigation, About and faculty/campus pages. Do not state one definitive current department total without reconciliation by the university.
- Full department profiles, faculty profiles, current office-holder directory, laboratory details, library services, hostel rules, transport routes and sports facilities are incomplete.

### Campus and recognition status
- HEC's listed Women Campus validity period ends 30 September 2025. Current renewal/recognition is not confirmed by this archive.
- Shangla should be described as historically associated with University of Swat and subsequently upgraded to the University of Shangla effective 1 August 2022, according to HEC.
- The Main Campus page identifies Charbagh; HEC's institutional profile separately lists G.T. Road, Odigram as contact information. Do not merge these addresses.

### Digital services and external references
- Portal landing-page descriptions do not establish that every service is currently operational or available to every user.
- No login, private student record or restricted portal area was accessed. Never imply that the chatbot can retrieve personal student records.
- External search-result-only references should be re-opened and independently verified before they are used as factual answer sources.

## RAG answer policy for unresolved questions

1. If a current deadline, fee, eligibility rule, programme availability or recognition status is not supported by a current official source, say that it is not confirmed in the knowledge base.
2. Link the user to the relevant official university page or admissions office; do not infer an answer from old notices.
3. If a source is historical or potentially outdated, state its date/context and do not present it as current.
4. If two official sources appear to conflict, explain the difference and preserve both source contexts rather than silently choosing one.
5. Cite the specific source URL and, where possible, the document title/date for every material factual answer.

## Priority work for the next collection cycle

2. Obtain the current approved fee schedule and programme-wise fee tables.
